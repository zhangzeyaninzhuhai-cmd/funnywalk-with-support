About FunnyWalk

Welcome to the official support page for FunnyWalk! FunnyWalk is a wacky, fun game where you balance on stilts, dodge traps, and conquer crazy courses. Get ready to laugh, wobble, and test your skills!

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: How do I play FunnyWalk?

Control your character on stilts, avoid obstacles, and try not to fall! Timing and balance are key.

Q2: Why is the game crashing?

Make sure your device meets the minimum iOS requirements and that you have the latest app version installed.
